NEON CYCLE

Your trail is a wall. Turn to trap the rivals — and don't ride into your own.

3D graphics couldn't start in this browser — Neon Cycle needs WebGL. Try a recent Chrome, Edge or Firefox on a device with graphics support.

How to Play

  • You ride a light-cycle on a neon grid. Your trail is a solid wall — and so is every rival's. Ride into any trail, a pillar or the arena edge and the run ends.
  • Turn with A / D or Left / Right — turns are sharp 90°, and they queue, so a fast double-tap turns twice at the next two corners.
  • Space toggles BOOST — press once to switch it on, press again to switch it off. Boosting is fast but drains the meter; the meter refills over time and gold boost cells top it up.
  • Trap the rivals by cutting them off — force one into your trail, its own trail, a pillar or the wall to bank its score. A rival that blunders on its own still banks, but a real cut-off pays a bonus. Trap two quickly and the combo pays more.
  • Speed ramps up as the run goes on, and the free space only ever shrinks. Survive until every rival is trapped to clear the arena.
  • Your best score is saved per arena — beat it. Clearing an arena unlocks the next one. Each clear earns a rank: S (under par), A (under 1.5× par), B (cleared).

Controls

  • A / D or Left / Right: turn 90° · Space: toggle boost · P: pause · M: mute · R: restart
  • Mouse / touch: click or tap the on-screen ◀ / ▶ buttons to turn, BOOST to toggle boost
  • Esc closes a panel · Enter confirms on menus
